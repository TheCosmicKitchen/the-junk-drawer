Turns. Each turn: see your roll and plan (nothing moves), press RUN (or Space) and watch the line work hands-off, then the foe takes its turn. Machines level up from the dice they handle (max 3 XP a turn each, so spread your dice): stars show the level, levelled machines work faster and hit harder. Real-time in the toolbar, or ?realtime, plays the old way.
Build first. You get 60 seconds before the Colossus moves (start early for bonus gears). Parts cost gears ⚙; belts are free. Earn gears by routing dice into the Gear Furnace (bottom right) or a Gear Mint, and from each new phase. Route around stone pillars; belts over glowing vents make dice hot, icy or +1. Removing a part refunds it in full while building, half after.
Ashcan sandbox. Pick a part from the palette and click floor tiles to place it (it stays picked; 1-5 re-pick recent parts). R or the wheel rotates. Right-click or Esc drops it. Time slows while you place. A gold star marks parts that answer the current phase.
Re-route. Drag across empty floor to lay belts; click a belt to turn it. Drag a machine to move it. Right-click removes anything. Dice that run off a belt heap up on the floor (6 per tile, then they tumble onward); lay a belt over a heap to scoop it back up.
Pick a foe. The Rust Colossus changes over four phases (flyers, armor, boulders). The Mite Mother sends swarms of little mites: go fast and wide, with splash. The Ironshell has armor 8: pump your dice up, and flood it when its shell opens. The Lockbox only takes damage from dice showing its lock face: sort, flip and reroll. Click gates, switches, silos and rockets to use them. Space raises Parry Shields. P pauses.
