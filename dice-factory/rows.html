DICE FACTORY

Three rows of card slots; dice ride through them. Turn your phone sideways.

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger board.

Rows. Your factory is three habitat rows of six card slots. Each turn your roll is dealt into the rows, and the dice ride each row left to right through its cards. A modifier changes a die and passes it on; a weapon or defense card takes it.
Habitats. Whatever reaches the end of a row does that row’s action: the Armory fires it at the foe (half damage), the Exchange turns it into gears, the Masonry into block. Each card in a row makes its end action 10% stronger. Cards with a coloured dot like one row and work 25% better there.
Flow and splitting. Router cards (teal) send dice to the row above or below: tap one to flip its rule, tap its arrow corner to send the other way. The Prism halves a die across two rows, the Tri-Splitter sends a third into every row, the Crusher makes a swarm of 1s. Deal (top bar, or tap a row’s name, or keys 1-3) pours the whole roll into one row instead of spreading it.
Building. Pick a card below and tap a slot. Slots further right cost more. Drag a card to move it; right-click or Scrap removes it (full refund on turn 1, half after). Cards level up from the dice they handle (3 xp a turn at most). Block lasts until the end of the foe’s turn. A gold star marks cards that answer the current phase.
The foe is a card too: its intent and countdown show what it will do. Slams and rocks hit your block first. The Mite Mother’s mites gnaw every turn; the Ironshell opens the turn before it slams; the Lockbox only really takes dice that show its lock face; in its Meltdown the Colossus buries slots. Space runs, P pauses. The Scrap Dummy is free play.
