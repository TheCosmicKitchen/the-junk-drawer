Bulk import

Paste or drop in a pile of recipes, check what was read, and add them all at once.

1. Bring in the recipes

Typed-up or copied text, several recipes at once (a line of --- between them helps but isn’t needed), Markdown, a family .json file, or a recipe web page saved as .html.

Drop files here, or (.txt, .md, .json, .html)

2. Check them

Fix a title or link name here. For anything else, open “Fix the text”, change it, and read it again.

    3. Add them to the box

    By hand: download the family file with these added, to commit

    Put it in recipes/data/, replacing the old one, then commit and push.
